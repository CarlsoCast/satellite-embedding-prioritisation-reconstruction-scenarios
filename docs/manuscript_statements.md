## Data availability

AlphaEarth Foundations embeddings and Dynamic World products were accessed through Google Earth Engine. Sentinel-2 MSI Level-2A imagery was used to derive NDVI and NDMI, the Bogotá digital terrain model was obtained through IDECA, and the institutional flood-hazard reference corresponds to the mapping used in Bogotá's 2021 Land Use Plan. Source data remain subject to their respective access and licensing conditions. Large prepared embeddings and intermediate rasters are not duplicated in the code repository; instructions describing their required structure and placement are provided with the archived code. Compact derived results needed to verify the reported summaries are included in the repository.
